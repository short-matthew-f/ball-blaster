# Ball Blaster — v0.3: Worlds, Scaling and the One Tree

Oct 11, 2026 · Approved direction. Extends DESIGN-v0.2.md (combat is unchanged). Owner decisions are marked **[decided]**; defaults the owner has not ruled on are marked **[default]**.

## Goals

- **Long progression:** many stages without hand-building each one. Target ≈ 150 runs (5–6 hours) of authored content, then endless.
- **One tree [decided]:** every upgrade on a single pannable web with the 8-ball at the centre. No tabs.
- **Grind [decided]:** no respec. Routing choices are permanent.

## Worlds × stages × loops

A **world** is the authored unit; **stages** inside it are generated; **loops** recycle everything with bigger numbers.

| World | Identity | Physics | Hazard | New enemies | Boss archetype |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Felt Hall | the baseline | friction 0.6 · decel 70 · bounce 0.75 | none | grunt, skirmisher, bouncer, bomber, broodmother, thornback, brute, cleric, hive | Big Stripe |
| 2 Ice Cavern | everything slides, cascades everywhere | 0.4 · 45 · 0.92 | ice patches: zero friction zones | frost | Rime Golem (cadre) |
| 3 Clockwork Works | the board moves you | 0.6 · 70 · 0.8 | conveyors: shift every body 24 px per enemy phase; a gear that spins charges off-line | cog (a rolling gear that ricochets), tinker (repairs: heals allies 2) | The Mainspring (moves, phase 2 reverses the conveyors) |
| 4 Bog | sticky ground parks you in reach | 0.9 · 110 · 0.6 | mud patches: a ball entering one stops dead | leech (steals HP on contact, heals itself), croaker (ranged, lobs over cover) | Mire Queen (summons, pulls the hero 40 px each phase) |
| 5 Ember Pit | stopping is dangerous | 0.5 · 60 · 0.8 | lava patches: 2 damage per enemy phase you stand in; bombers common | cinder (explodes on *any* contact, 1 to neighbours), magmaw (brute that leaves a lava trail) | The Kiln (phases ignite the board: more lava each phase) |
| 6 Neon Casino | greed | 0.6 · 70 · 0.95 | slot bumpers: hit three in one shot → jackpot cash; chip enemies pay double but call reinforcements | dealer (shuffles enemy positions each phase), bouncer-elite | The House (cadre of dealers; moves; every trait) |

**[default]** six worlds at launch, played in this order (linear). Each world ships with sprites, a hazard, two enemy kinds, a boss, and a branch on the tree.

**Stages within a world** (4 each, generated by `makeStage(world, slot)`):

| Slot | Adds | Boss trait |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | the world's identity, plain | phases |
| 2 | one modifier | phases + cadre |
| 3 | one modifier, elites from wave 2 | phases + moves |
| 4 | two modifiers, the world's full roster weight | all traits |

**Modifier pool** (picked deterministically per stage from the global index, never the same modifier twice in a world): ranged-heavy · double bombers · extra elite · thin rails (bounce 0.6) · big rack (+1 enemy per wave) · early boss (boss after 2 waves) · wardens common · swift (all Advance +50 %) · hardy (+25 % HP, −20 % count) · dark (intents revealed only within reach) · hive world (one hive per wave) · frost world (one frost per wave, any world).

**Loops [default]:** after stage 24, loop 1 replays worlds 1–6 with scaling continuing, two modifiers on every stage, bosses with all traits, and Overtime cash at 1×. Loops are the endless tail; the Challenge toggle question stays open.

## Scaling

Global stage index `n` (0 = Felt 1, 23 = Casino 4, 24+ = loops).

| Quantity | Per stage | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Enemy HP | × 1.28ⁿ | |
| Enemy attack | × 1.10ⁿ | slower than HP because hero HP grows slower than hero damage |
| Cash per kill, wave bonus, boss cash | × 1.28ⁿ | same base as HP, so each stage's economy feels like stage 1 |
| Emeralds per first boss kill | 3 + n | repeat decay as now |
| Tree ring n node costs | × 1.28ⁿ | rings are priced in the cash of the stage that unlocks them |

**Arrival ratio:** on arriving at stage n with everything affordable from stage n−1 bought, the player should be at ~60 % of the power needed to clear it, and ~6 runs of stage-n cash should close the gap. The multi-stage progression bot (newSave → play → buy greedily → advance when a boss dies) checks **runs-to-clear ∈ [4, 9] for every stage** and is the release gate; if a stage drifts, the exponents move, not the stage.

## The One Tree

**Shape.** A web on a virtual 640 × 640 canvas, the 8-ball (root, always owned) at the centre. Four trunks radiate: **Power** north, **Vitality** south, **Tactics** west, **Combo** east. Trunks cross-link every ring so a build can reach a neighbouring trunk's node by a side path. **Emerald keystones** sit on the diagonals at ring junctions (ball types, bumpers, crates, chests, shrine, stray emeralds). Node spacing 40 px; nodes 22 px.

**Rules.**
- A node is purchasable when any linked node is owned (level ≥ 1). The root counts as owned.
- **Stat nodes have levels [decided]**, up to 5, shown as pips; each level costs × 1.5 the last. **Unlocks and keystones are one-shot.**
- **No respec [decided].**
- The tree is **not** the stage map [decided]; stages are chosen on a separate map screen.

**Rings.**
- **Ring 1** (3 nodes touching the root): Damage I, Max HP I, Aim I. That is the entire visible choice on run 1.
- **Rings 2–3** (the core, ~30 nodes, hand-placed): everything in today's six tabs, re-laid as trunks. Tier I values are today's values.
- **World rings:** reaching a world (its first stage unlocked) grows a **graft** at the tip of the trunk nearest its theme (Ice → Vitality, Clockwork → Tactics, Bog → Combo, Ember → Power, Casino → the Combo/Power diagonal, Felt → a small graft on Vitality available from the start). A graft has: the world's **counter node** (grip on ice, anti-mud in the bog, heat shield in the pit…), a **cash node** for that world, a **tier-up stat node** (Damage II = +10 %/level, Max HP II = +10 %/level, etc.), and a **capstone** one-shot global perk (requires the other three maxed). Graft costs are ring-priced for that world's stages.
- **Loop rings:** procedural, one small cluster per trunk per loop (+8 % damage, +8 % HP, +5 % cash, +1 combo reward), ring-priced. Endless.

**Disclosure.** Owned nodes: full. Adjacent to owned: full, with cost. Two steps out: dim silhouette, no text. Beyond: fog. Locked grafts show as a stub at the trunk tip reading the unlock condition ("REACH ICE CAVERN"). Pips and the yellow can-afford dot as today.

**Screen.** Drag to pan (tap slop 10 px as the shop has now), a 2× zoom-out toggle for overview, a recentre button, tap a node → bottom panel (name, LV n>n+1, next value, cost with coin/gem, BUY), the path from the nearest owned node to the tapped node highlighted, affordable nodes glow. The UPGRADE buttons on title and summary keep the dot.

**Data.** `UpgradeNode` gains `x, y, links: string[]`, `ring: number`, `kind: 'stat' | 'unlock' | 'keystone' | 'capstone' | 'counter'`, and `graft?: WorldId`; `tree/pos/requires` go away (adjacency replaces `requires`; capstones keep an explicit "siblings maxed" gate). `buildStats` is unchanged. The core nodes are a hand-written table; grafts and loop rings come from templates.

## Stage map screen

Worlds as rows (name, tint), four stage pips each (locked / open / best wave 0–3 / boss killed), the current loop shown as a badge, next locked stage greyed with "beat the boss of …". Tap a pip to select it; START RUN as now. Replaces the prev/next arrows.

## Save

`current` becomes a stage id string (`w1s1` … `w6s4`, loops `l1w1s1`), `unlocked` the highest global index reached, `bestWave` keyed by stage id, `levels` keyed by node id. **Migration from v0.2:** keep cash, emeralds, stats and settings; refund every owned node at its total paid cost and clear levels (the tree is new, the grind restarts from a fat wallet). Ice unlock carries over as `unlocked ≥ 4`.

## Build order

1. **Tree + map UI** (meta: node model, core table, graft/loop templates, migration; UI: tree screen, map screen) — on the current two stages first.
2. **Stage generator + scaling + modifiers + boss trait escalation + multi-stage bot.** Felt and Ice become worlds 1–2 with four stages each (eight stages immediately).
3. **World 3, Clockwork Works** — spec, hazards, enemies, boss, sprites, graft. Then Bog, Ember Pit, Neon Casino, each as one build cycle.
4. **Loops.**

## Decision log

| Decision | Reason |
|---|---|
| Worlds authored, stages generated | content cost scales with worlds (6), not stages (24+) |
| Geometric scaling with cash tracking HP | every stage's economy feels like stage 1 (v0.1 doc rule); the bot tunes exponents |
| One tree with adjacency, levels on stat nodes, no respec, separate map **[decided]** | routing is the meta-game; permanence makes it a grind by design |
| Grafts grow the tree per world | progression is visible on the tree itself; sub-trees stop being tabs |
| Rings priced in stage cash | the tree's geography carries the scaling, not infinite level counters |
| Migration refunds node spend | the tree is new; the player keeps what they earned |
